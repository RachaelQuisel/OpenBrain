# Turn meeting notes into clear decisions, action items, unresolved questions, and follow-up material

This skill helps you turn meeting notes into clear decisions, action items, unresolved questions, and follow-up material.

## What to share

State the question or decision, share the sources you want considered, and identify the intended reader.

## How to start

Select this skill and describe your request. The assistant uses the information and tools available in that conversation.
